# Asset provenance

All people and the architectural scene are fictional and generated using the built-in image generation tool. Portraits are optimized as WebP; the hero is a 2400 × 1350 JPEG.

## Hero prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: full-bleed website hero for a luxury international advisory firm
Primary request: original photorealistic editorial architectural photograph of an explicitly fictional modern international financial district at blue hour. No claim of a specific real city.
Scene/backdrop: monumental elegant glass and dark stone skyscrapers seen from a low street-level viewpoint; restrained, timeless contemporary financial architecture, clean disciplined vertical lines and subtle illuminated office windows.
Composition/framing: very wide landscape 2400 by 1350 or equivalent 16:9 composition. Building mass concentrated in the center and right two-thirds. The left third is dark, low-detail negative space suitable for a large white headline. Strong upward perspective, generous dark sky; no people, no cars, no street signage.
Lighting/mood: blue hour, deep charcoal blacks, very muted blue-gray ambient light, subtle kings-red crimson reflected light on a few glass and stone surfaces. Sophisticated restrained atmosphere; soft believable reflections, dramatic scale.
Style/medium: high-end editorial architecture photography, realistic glass, stone and metal textures, precise lines, natural atmospheric depth, photograph rather than rendering.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no iconic recognizable landmark, no overly futuristic science-fiction towers, no glowing neon signs, no cartoon, no composite collage.

## Typography

Space Grotesk by Florian Karsten, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The locally available variable font is bundled in public/assets/space-grotesk.ttf. Georgia and Times New Roman are system fallbacks and are not redistributed.

## Regional portrait additions
Seven original fictional professional portraits were generated with the built-in image_gen tool to expand African and Asian pools. Exact prompts are stored in original-assets/regional-portrait-prompts.json and original-assets/regional-more-portrait-prompts.json. Optimized website assets are in public/assets/portraits/; PNG originals remain in original-assets/portraits/. Country identities share these regional pools; the portraits do not verify real personnel.

## OpenRouter portrait pool
192 new fictional professional portraits were generated through OpenRouter using black-forest-labs/flux.2-klein-4b, provider Black Forest Labs, 768x1024 JPEG originals. The website serves optimized 600x800 WebP images. Twelve regional pools contain eight men and eight women each. Country allocation rotates pairs through the appropriate pool; identities, names and specializations remain unchanged. Portraits are reused across countries.

Exact prompts, seeds, image dimensions and per-image API-reported costs are in scripts/data/portrait-library.json. Credentials are read from OPENROUTER_API_KEY only and never stored in the repository. The generator has a $5 total cap and a resumable private ledger under IR_PORTRAIT_WORK_DIR outside the repository. Originals are retained in that private working directory. Endpoint reference: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/images/models/black-forest-labs/flux.2-klein-4b/endpoints . Generation API: https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/multimodal/image-generation .

Run with node --import tsx scripts/generate-portraits.ts; --pilot limits the initial run to one man and one woman per region. Re-running resumes without regenerating completed or uncertain attempts.


## Individual country portrait additions
100 new fictional country delegates were generated through OpenRouter with bytedance-seed/seedream-5-0-lite, provider Seed, at 2K resolution and 3:4 aspect ratio. Optimized 768x1024 WebP assets are in public/assets/portraits/individual/. Each filename and manifest entry refers to one stable delegate ID; image assignments are checked against the roster's country and gender. The earlier ten-image review batch uses the same model and some of those portraits are retained for delegates present in the revised allocation.

Prompts vary age, facial anatomy, hair, glasses, facial hair, clothes, colour, environment, expression and pose. They request imagined individuals with country and regional context; they do not verify real people or national identity. Exact prompts, seeds, traits and costs are in original-assets/delegate-portrait-manifest.json. This batch cost $3.50. The accounted cumulative OpenRouter cost is $6.79 under the user's approved $7 cap. Portraits still sourced from regional pools may be reused between countries; the 100 individual additions are not reused.
